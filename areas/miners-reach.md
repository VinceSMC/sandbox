# Miner’s Reach

**Description**
<br>Miner’s Reach is located North of the build and contains the Blacksmith and other mining-related services.

**Structures**
<br>All structures currently located in this area are listed below.
> [!NOTE]
> The numbers in this table correspond to the numbers displayed above each structure on the build server. A dash means the structure is not currently numbered.

| # | Structure |
|---:|-----------|
| 21 | [The Blacksmith](../structures/21-the-blacksmith.md) |

**NPCs**
<br>These NPCs belong to the area but are not currently assigned to a numbered structure.

| # | Name | Location | Notes | NPC ID |
|---:|------|----------|-------|--------|

| 2 | Redstone Merchant | Miner’s Reach | Opens the Redstone Merchant shop. | `loc-village-redstone-merchant` |
| 3 | Tobin | Miner’s Reach | Opens the Stockkeeper shop, which includes Coal for Minion fuel. | `loc-village-stockkeeper` |
