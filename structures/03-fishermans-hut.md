# Fisherman's Hut

**Structure type**
<br>`Fisherman's Hut`

**Area**
<br>[Fisher’s Haven](../areas/fishers-haven.md)

**Description**
<br>Serves as the main location for fishing-related content. A merchant at the dock buys and sells fish.
<br>The current structure is approved and can be used in the final build. It should sit within a water-rich, strongly fishing-themed environment, with a clear dock for the merchant. Newer Minecraft materials are welcome where they improve the structure without changing its overall identity.
