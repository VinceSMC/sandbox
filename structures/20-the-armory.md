# The Armory

**Structure type**
<br>`Armory`

**Area**
<br>[Combat Settlement](../areas/combat-settlement.md)

**Description**
<br>Allows players to purchase weapons and armor through the Weaponsmith and Armorer.
<br>The current structure does not fit the intended style, so a redesign would be preferred. The new Armory should complement the Library so the Combat Settlement feels intentionally planned and visually connected. The interior can give both merchants their own recognizable counter or display area.
