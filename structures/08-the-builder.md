# The Builder

**Structure type**
<br>`Builder`

**Area**
<br>[Village](../areas/village.md)

**Description**
<br>Houses merchants for building blocks, colored materials, and redstone components.
<br>The exterior direction and use of newer materials are approved. The entrance could use more depth and detail; an additional facade in a contrasting style is one possible direction. The white building beside it may be adjusted or replaced, but it should continue to frame the route into the next area. The interior can give each merchant a recognizable section while keeping all three services within one cohesive building. The complete structure should also blend more naturally into the Village through its palette, terrain, paths, and neighboring buildings. The exact entrance treatment is still open.
