# Town Hall

**Structure type**
<br>`Town Hall`

**Area**
<br>[Village (Center)](../areas/village.md)

**Description**
<br>Acts as the village's main progression and information building. Players can upgrade their personal account or island, spend Gems in the Gem Shop, learn more about Skyblock through the Wiki NPC, and access the online store through the Store NPC.
<br><br>The Town Hall should feel like one of the most important civic buildings in the village. Provide enough interior space for its different services and organize them into clear, easy-to-find areas, with upgrades treated as the primary feature. The exact internal layout is still open.
