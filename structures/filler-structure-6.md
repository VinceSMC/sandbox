# Filler Structure (6)

**Structure type**
<br>`Farm Stand`

**Area**
<br>[Farm](../areas/farm.md)

**Description**
<br>Provides the Farm Merchant with a small shop inside the Farm area, keeping farming-related goods close to the crops and animals.
<br>This can be a compact stand or small filler building with a clear service counter and a few displays of farm goods. Its exact design is open, as long as it feels naturally integrated into the Farm.
