# Filler Structure (3)

**Structure type**
<br>`Filler`

**Area**
<br>[Village](../areas/village.md)

**Description**
<br>Forms part of the outer Village border and creates an additional entrance or exit.
<br>These structures have no direct gameplay function and are flexible. They may be redesigned or replaced as long as they continue to form a complete Village edge and a clear secondary entrance or exit.

<br>No NPC is currently assigned to this structure.
