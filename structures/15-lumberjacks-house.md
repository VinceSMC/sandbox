# Lumberjack's House

**Structure type**
<br>`Lumberjack's House`

**Area**
<br>[The Forest](../areas/the-forest.md)

**Description**
<br>Serves as the Lumberjack's home and quest location within The Forest.
<br>The Lumberjack should stand outside the structure and give players lumberjack and foraging-related quests. Other wood-related services can sit nearby in their own Forest structures, so this build can stay focused on the Lumberjack. The exact surrounding details are still open.
