# Cosmetics

**Structure type**
<br>`Cosmetics`

**Area**
<br>[Village](../areas/village.md)

**Description**
<br>Provides access to cosmetic content. Players unlock cosmetics in the underground area and purchase them with Gems from the Cosmetic Shop upstairs.
<br>The current structure can be replaced with a new design that gives the cosmetics feature a more distinctive identity. It should continue to support both the underground unlock area and the upstairs shop. The exact architectural theme is still open.
